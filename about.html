About Maruu de' Shop

Welcome to Maruu de' Shop, your trusted local source for premium, stylish, and durable phone cases right here in PMU.

What sets this shop apart isn't just the quality of the products, but the story behind the screen. Maruu de' Shop is proudly created, developed, and managed entirely by a single student, Georges Chan Teck Chiang.

As an Information Technology student, I built this entire e-commerce platform from scratch to blend my passion for web development with a genuine desire to provide great products to my local community. When you shop here, you aren't buying from a faceless corporation—you are directly supporting a student's hustle and academic journey.

Thank you for stopping by, and please feel free to reach out via WhatsApp if you have any questions or need help finding the perfect case for your device!
